Keep a private record of every fishing outing. Log species, lure, count, largest weight and length, a place, notes and a catch photo. Save exact coordinates by hand, on the built-in offline atlas, or with optional location access. Save weather alongside a catch. Recent dates use hourly data from the ECCC weather model where available. For older dates, the app uses the NASA POWER archive. Explore your own species calendar and lure statistics. Export CSV, a complete JSON backup, a catch card or an annual PDF report. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read local cached information with timestamps. Choose a file to open, or send a copy directly to someone. Changes stay on this device until you share again. No purchases, subscriptions, ads or tracking. The atlas shows coastlines and borders and is not a detailed navigation chart.
